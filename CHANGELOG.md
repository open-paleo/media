# Changelog

Changes to the media output, `dist/media.json`, newest first. Images added or replaced are not
listed.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-21)

- Outlines may be licensed CC BY 2.0 (`CC-BY-2.0`); the site needs its name and address for
  credit lines

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-18)

- Outlines may stand for a genus as well as a clade: `outlines` is keyed by either. A genus with
  its own outline shows it rather than its clade's
- Outline files are in folders by first letter, such as `outlines/t/titanosauria.svg`; use each
  outline's `file` rather than building the path

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-18)

- Outlines give the taxon they were drawn from (`depicts`) and how the cleaned file differs from
  the original (`changes`), for captions and credit lines
- Licenses may also be `PDM-1.0`, `CC-BY-SA-3.0` and `CC-BY-SA-4.0`
- The first twelve outlines, from Dinosauria to Pachycephalosauria

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-18)

- First version: outlines keyed by clade, each with its file, caption, creator, source and license
